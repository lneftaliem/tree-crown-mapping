# Austin HOLC redlining polygons

`austin_redlining_data.json` holds the 18 Home Owners' Loan Corporation (HOLC)
residential security grade polygons for Austin, Texas (1937), as GeoJSON in
WGS 84. It is the input to `generate_redlining_figures.py`.

The polygons come from **Mapping Inequality: Redlining in New Deal America**
(Digital Scholarship Lab, University of Richmond), which distributes them under a
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The file is redistributed here unchanged under the same terms.
Please cite:

> Nelson, R. K., Winling, L., et al. Mapping Inequality: Redlining in New Deal
> America. In R. K. Nelson & E. L. Ayers (eds.), *American Panorama: An Atlas of
> United States History*. Digital Scholarship Lab, University of Richmond.
> https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
